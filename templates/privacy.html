Privacy

Your pod's data belongs to your pod.

What is stored

Account identity, pod membership, decks, game records, live-game state, and first-party activation events. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes; invite and recap tokens are also stored only as hashes.

Public recaps

Recaps are private unless a member explicitly publishes one. Public pages omit usernames, decklists, private notes, and links to other games. A published link can be revoked.

Your controls

Account export and deletion/anonymization are available from your profile. Operational backups may retain encrypted copies for a limited restoration window.

Contact and processors

Before public production launch this notice must be completed with the operator's legal identity, contact address, hosting/email processors, retention windows, and lawful bases. This beta text is product disclosure, not a completed legal notice.
